About Little Lemon

Little Lemon is a family-owned restaurant dedicated to authentic flavors and warm hospitality. Based in Chicago, Illinois, Little Lemon is a family-owned Mediterranean restaurant,
focused on traditional recipes served with a modern twist. The chefs draw inspiration from Italian, Greek, and Turkish culture and have a menu of 12–15 items that they rotate seasonally.
The restaurant has a rustic and relaxed atmosphere with moderate prices, making it a popular place for a meal any time of the day.
